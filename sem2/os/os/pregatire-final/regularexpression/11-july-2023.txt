RO: Ce puteţi face ca programator pentru a preveni deadlock-urile?
EN: What can you do as a software developer to prevent deadlocks?

Answer:
A deadlock can be caused by many factors such as mutual exclusion, hold & wait, no preemption and circular wait. The best approch we can adopt to prevent a deadlock is to unlock and lock resources in the same order to prevent circular wait and not to have cicles in the allocation graph of resources.
